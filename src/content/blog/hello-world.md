Welcome! This is the blog attached to my academic site. I'll use it for research
notes, short write-ups on explainable AI and process mining, and the occasional
walkthrough of a method or paper.

## What to expect

- **Research notes** — half-formed ideas, reading summaries, and things I want to
  remember.
- **Method walkthroughs** — occasionally with interactive, scroll-driven visuals
  (the site ships a scrollytelling template built for exactly that).
- **Meta posts** like this one.

## How this is built

The site is a static [Astro](https://astro.build) project.
